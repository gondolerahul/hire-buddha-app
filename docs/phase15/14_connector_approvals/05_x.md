## 1. What X checks

X doesn't review apps before launch. It enforces afterwards: an app or account that breaks the Developer Agreement, the Developer Guidelines or the automation rules is rate-limited, suspended or banned, and an app suspension stays even when the account is reinstated. The approval that does exist is **prior approval for AI-generated replies**, through X's Policy Support form.

## 2. X's rules that bind the product

* **User-initiated only:** an interaction must be something the authenticated person asked for. Replies only to people who engaged first, at most one per interaction. DMs only after the person wrote first, with an easy opt-out. No auto-likes.
* **AI-generated replies need X's prior approval**, and must still follow every rule (no unsolicited mentions, no impersonating a person).
* **Automated accounts** carry the *Automated* label, say in the bio that a bot runs them and who operates it, and link to a human-managed account.
* **No spam or manipulation:** no identical content across accounts, no bulk posting, no uninvited @mentions, no keyword-triggered replies. One app per use case.
* **No training** of AI or ML models on X data.
* **Deletion:** delete X content within 24 hours when it is deleted on X or X asks; delete all X data within 10 business days after API access ends. X may audit once a year, so keep records.
* Commercial use needs a paid tier (pay per use counts).

## 4. Common problems and how they were resolved

Sources: X's developer guidelines (official), X developer community threads, practitioner write-ups (§5). Reddit could not be opened from this environment.

| Problem | Usual cause | What fixed it |
|---|---|---|
| App suspended for automation | Repetitive or duplicate posts, keyword-triggered replies, unsolicited mentions | Stop the pattern (X-E1, X-E3), then appeal; developers report the account reinstated while the app stays suspended, so ask for the app separately |
| AI reply feature refused or suspended | Launched without X's prior approval | X-05 before launch |
| Posting fails with a payment error | Credits used up or the spending limit hit | X-04; X-E5 makes it a clear error |
| 429 Too Many Requests | Per-user or per-app limit | X-E6 |
