# Deleting your data — Buddha Cognitive Lab

**Last updated:** [LAST UPDATED DATE]

This page is about the data you gave **this website**, buddhalab.in. If you use **HireBuddha**, or a business contacted you through HireBuddha, go to [hirebuddha.com/data-deletion](https://hirebuddha.com/data-deletion): it explains how to disconnect accounts, close a HireBuddha account, and delete data that came from Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google and other services.

## What you can ask us to delete

* What you sent through the contact form, by e-mail or by phone.
* Your newsletter subscription (or use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter).
* The details you gave to download a whitepaper or join a waitlist.
* A job application.

## How to ask

Write to [privacy@buddhalab.in](mailto:privacy@buddhalab.in) from the e-mail address you used, saying what you want deleted. If you contacted us by phone or from another address, tell us the details you gave so we can find them.

## What happens

* We acknowledge your request within 72 hours and complete it within 30 days.
* We delete the data from our systems and ask the service providers that hold it for us to delete it too. Copies in backups expire on their normal schedule (logs within 30 days).
* We may keep what the law requires us to keep (for example, records of a contract or invoice), and will tell you what and why.
* We may ask you to confirm your identity before deleting anything.
