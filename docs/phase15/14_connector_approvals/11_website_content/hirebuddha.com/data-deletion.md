# Deleting your data from HireBuddha

**Last updated:** [LAST UPDATED DATE]

HireBuddha is run by Buddha Cognitive Lab Private Limited. This page explains how to delete data from HireBuddha, what is deleted, what we must keep, and how long it takes. Our [Privacy Policy](https://hirebuddha.com/privacy) has the full detail.

Choose what you want to do:

1. [Disconnect an account you connected](#1-disconnect-an-account) (Gmail, Outlook, a Facebook Page, Instagram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Google Ads, GitHub, a mailbox…)
2. [Delete data that came from Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp](#2-facebook-instagram-and-whatsapp)
3. [Close your business account and delete everything](#3-close-your-business-account)
4. [Remove yourself as a user of a business's account](#4-remove-yourself-as-a-user)
5. [Ask a business that uses HireBuddha to delete your data](#5-you-are-a-customer-of-a-business-that-uses-hirebuddha)
6. [Delete what you gave on our website](#6-website-enquiries-and-pragya)

## 1. Disconnect an account

In HireBuddha, an owner or administrator of your business opens **Connections**, chooses the account, selects **Manage**, then **Disconnect**.

What happens at once:

* HireBuddha's access is revoked at the provider where the provider allows it (Google, X, GitHub; for Facebook Pages, Instagram and WhatsApp, HireBuddha also stops receiving their updates).
* The stored access tokens, or the stored password, are deleted.
* The mail, comments, messages and other events HireBuddha stored from that account are deleted.
* Automations that depend on the account are paused.

What remains: the history of what your AI employees did with that account stays in your workspace until you delete it or close your account, always within each provider's limits: LinkedIn members' activity for at most 48 hours and their profile details for at most 24 hours; X content no more than 24 hours after it is deleted on X, and all X data within 10 business days of disconnecting; YouTube data within 7 days of disconnecting.

Some providers do not let an app revoke its own access. To remove HireBuddha at the provider too:

| Provider | Where |
|---|---|
| Google (Gmail, Drive, YouTube, Google Ads) | [Google Account → Third-party access](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) |
| Microsoft (Outlook, Microsoft 365) | Users: [myapps.microsoft.com](https://myapps.microsoft.com). Administrators: Microsoft Entra admin centre → Enterprise applications |
| Facebook Pages and Instagram | Facebook → Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations |
| WhatsApp Business | Your business portfolio's WhatsApp account settings → Partners |
| LinkedIn | LinkedIn → Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services |
| X | X → Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions |
| GitHub | GitHub → Settings → Applications → Installed GitHub Apps |
| Mailbox (app password) | Your mail provider's app password page (Gmail: [myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords)) |

## 2. Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp

You can delete the data HireBuddha received through your Facebook account in either of two ways:

* **In Facebook:** open Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find **HireBuddha**, remove it and ask for your data to be deleted. Meta tells HireBuddha, and HireBuddha disconnects every Page, Instagram account and WhatsApp number you connected and deletes their tokens and the messages, comments and other events stored from them. Meta then gives you a **confirmation code** and a link to a page that shows what we deleted.
* **By e-mail:** write to [privacy@hirebuddha.com](mailto:privacy@hirebuddha.com) from the e-mail address of your HireBuddha account, or with the name of the Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number. We delete the data and reply with confirmation within 30 days.

If you have a confirmation code, its status page is at the link Meta gave you.

## 3. Close your business account

An owner of the business opens **Settings → Data & closing** and asks to close the account. You can also ask us at [privacy@hirebuddha.com](mailto:privacy@hirebuddha.com), or through the partner that manages your account.

1. **Grace period (30 days).** The account becomes read-only. Your AI employees, calls, automations and apps stop. You can download your invoices and an **export** of your data (records, contacts, campaigns, AI employees, documents and files), or change your mind.
2. **Erasure.** At the end of the grace period, within one working day, we erase your business's database, files, recordings, connected account tokens, AI employees and their memory, users and access, and destroy the encryption key that belongs to your business. You receive a certificate of erasure by e-mail.
3. **What we keep, and why.** Indian tax and company law require us to keep invoices, credit notes, payment and wallet records and our audit trail for **8 years**. We keep them in a separate archive and remove personal details they do not need.
4. **Backups.** Copies in backups expire on fixed schedules: database restore points 7 days, weekly backups 5 weeks, deleted files 14 days, logs 30 days. Because your business's encryption key is destroyed, any encrypted data left in a backup can no longer be read.

Unused credits lapse when an account is closed.

## 4. Remove yourself as a user

Ask an administrator of your business to remove you from its HireBuddha account (**Settings → Team** [CHECK THE MENU NAME]). If you cannot, write to [privacy@hirebuddha.com](mailto:privacy@hirebuddha.com) from your account's e-mail address. Your sign-in details are deleted; the business's records of the work you did belong to the business.

## 5. You are a customer of a business that uses HireBuddha

If a business talked to you, called you, e-mailed you or messaged you through HireBuddha, that **business** decides what happens to your data. Ask the business to delete it: HireBuddha gives each business a tool to find and erase a person's data across its workspace. If you cannot reach the business, or it does not answer, write to [privacy@hirebuddha.com](mailto:privacy@hirebuddha.com) with the business's name and your e-mail address or phone number. We will pass your request to the business and help it act on it.

## 6. Website enquiries and Pragya

To delete what you sent through hirebuddha.com's contact form or your conversations with Pragya, write to [privacy@hirebuddha.com](mailto:privacy@hirebuddha.com) from the e-mail address you used, or tell us the details you gave.

## Timing and verification

We acknowledge requests within 72 hours and complete them within 30 days, often much sooner. We may ask you to confirm your identity, or that you act for the business, before deleting anything. If we cannot delete something because the law requires us to keep it, we will say what and why.
