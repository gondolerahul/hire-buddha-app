## Vocabulary and policy defaults for the draft

**Process** means an end-to-end business workflow, **Agent** a role requiring judgment, **Skill** reusable instructions/scripts/assets with a contract, **Action** an optional stable wrapper, and **Tool** the controlled executable primitive. **Workflow entity** and **business record** are different: an Invoice is a record, not an Agent entity. An organizational scope is a permission/budget/reporting boundary, not necessarily a new kind of AI entity.

In the optional six-level hierarchy, **Graph** is the whole business and **Loop** a department/function. This organizational Graph is distinct from a workflow dependency DAG and from the tenant's evidence-based knowledge graph. Shared Skills are references reused across roles; visual containment must not require copying their executable versions or extra LLM calls at every level.

Autonomy is scoped to a capability and action category. A0 observes/drafts internally; A1 requires human approval for external effects; A2 allows approved bounded actions with exception review; A3 adds audited delegated operation within the same hard limits; A4 adds governed change proposals and testing. None bypass permissions, policy, consent, budgets, or mandatory checkpoints. Internal autonomy does not grant broad access to internal data or permission to alter sensitive records.

The existing voice `campaigns` feature and a **GTM Campaign** are separate concepts. The wallet **cost ledger** measures platform use; the business **accounting ledger** records the tenant's commerce. Platform usage costs and third-party ad/payout commitments require separate limits. Observed revenue, estimates, attribution, and forecast are labeled separately.
