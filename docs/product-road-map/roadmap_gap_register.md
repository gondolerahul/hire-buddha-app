This register continues the audit tradition of Blueprint v2 §0.1 (which closed v1's G1–G12): it records every mistake, gap, and improvement found when auditing all three Phase 2 documents against each other and against the demands of a real deployment. Each finding keeps a stable ID; close a finding by setting **Status** and **Resolved in** (doc + section or commit).

**Status vocabulary:** `Open` · `In analysis` · `In progress` · `Resolved` · `Won't fix (rationale)`
**Severity:** `Critical` — breaks the concept or blocks deployment · `High` — must fix before the docs are relied on · `Medium` — fix in next revision · `Low` — cleanup.

---

## Overall Verdict

The conceptual architecture is strong — the five-tier hierarchy, the one-loop doctrine, the Karuna membrane, and the autonomy ladder are better thought through than most agentic-platform designs. The findings fall into three failure classes:

1. **Internal contradictions and count errors** that survived v2's own audit (register A).
2. **Blueprint v2 "closed" its gaps by referencing platform mechanisms the technical doc never defines** — the gap moved, it didn't close (registers B, D).
3. **The docs describe an end-state as shipped GA fact, with no actual roadmap** from the current codebase to that end-state (register F — the meta-gap).
