Mobile dialer · Android · v2 design

The rep's phone, made calm.

Twenty-six high-fidelity screens for the HireBuddha conference dialer, rebuilt on the Buddha Cognitive Lab design system. Every screen is real product state — drawn from the shipped Kotlin, the backend contract and the ADR — not lorem. Gold marks nine screens that do not exist in the app today.

412 × 892 dp · Pixel class Dark canvas #0a0908 Gold #edab48 Space Grotesk / Hanken Grotesk / JetBrains Mono Lucide-style 1.75px icons

I · Arrival

Brand first. A rep installs a sideloaded APK from a link — the first three seconds have to say who this is from.

II · The day

A rep opens this app twenty times a day. The first screen should answer “what now?”, not “which campaign?”

III · The run

Where the product earns its money. The rep is holding the phone, often standing, often outdoors, and has about two seconds to read each state. These eight screens carry the whole orchestrator.

IV · Afterwards

What the rep and their manager look at once the calling stops.

V · Dialer duties

Holding ROLE_DIALER means Android expects a real phone app. These three surfaces are obligations — but they are also where the brand meets a rep's everyday calls.
